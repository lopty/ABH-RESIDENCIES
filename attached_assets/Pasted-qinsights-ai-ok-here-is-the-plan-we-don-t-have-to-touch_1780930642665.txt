Your instinct is correct.

If I were brought in as the SEO/GEO lead for QInsights and was told:

"Don't touch the application code. Build authority, rankings, GEO visibility, and competitive depth."

I would not start with blog articles.

I would start with entity authority architecture.

Current Problem

From what I can see, QInsights has a good product page and positioning around AI-assisted qualitative research. The issue is not the product. The issue is authority and topical depth.

Right now Google, ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity need answers to questions like:

What is QInsights?
Is QInsights a qualitative analysis software?
Is QInsights a replacement for NVivo?
Is QInsights better than Atlas.ti?
Is QInsights a market research tool?
Is QInsights an interview analysis platform?
Is QInsights an academic research software?
Is QInsights trustworthy?
Who uses QInsights?
What is the difference between QInsights and ChatGPT?

The website currently doesn't have enough indexed evidence to dominate those conversations.
