ResidenceAbuDhabi.com -- Full SEO Strategy
What the Site Is Right Now
AB Residencies is a luxury real estate specialist focused on one primary product: Jumeirah Residences Al Maryah Island -- the first Jumeirah-branded residential project in Abu Dhabi. Single developer. Single project. Golden Visa eligible. Off-plan.
The meta is clean, OG tags are set, canonical is there. That is better than most. But the site has one fatal problem: it is a one-product microsite competing against Bayut, Property Finder, NAS Luxury, OIA Properties, and every major agency in Abu Dhabi that has hundreds of pages of real estate content indexed. It has no authority, no depth, no topical coverage, and no reason for Google or any AI to recommend it over those giants.
The strategy is the same playbook as QInsights but adapted for real estate. You build depth, entity authority, and programmatic pages around the niche this agency actually owns -- luxury branded residences in Abu Dhabi -- rather than trying to compete broadly against Bayut.

The Niche to Own
Do not try to rank for "Abu Dhabi real estate." That is Bayut's territory and it is unwinnable without years and millions. Instead, own these specific lanes:
Branded residences Abu Dhabi -- this is a fast-growing category with real search volume and relatively thin competition in terms of deep content. Most results are either news articles or broad agency pages, not authoritative resource hubs.
Jumeirah Residences Al Maryah Island -- this is the exact product. Own every keyword variation of this project name before any other agency does.
Golden Visa property Abu Dhabi -- massive search intent from investors globally. Specific, transactional, high-converting.
Off-plan investment Abu Dhabi -- investors searching for yield, capital appreciation, payment plans.

The Key Difference Between This and QInsights
QInsights needs topical authority across a software category. The content depth is the moat.
ResidenceAbuDhabi.com needs transactional trust and local authority. The buyer is making a multi-million dirham decision. Before they call, they will have read 10 to 20 pages. The site that has the most complete, honest, specific information about Jumeirah Residences Al Maryah Island and the Abu Dhabi branded residence market is the one that gets the inquiry.
So every page here must do three things the competitors are not doing:

Give real numbers (price per sqft, service charge estimates, rental yield projections, payment plan structures)
Give real process (how Golden Visa works step by step, how off-plan purchase works in Abu Dhabi, what happens at handover)
Give real comparisons (why Al Maryah Island over Saadiyat, why Jumeirah Residences over other branded projects in the same price band)

That depth is what ranks. That depth is what converts.
