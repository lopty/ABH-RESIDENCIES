## Why Al Maryah Island

Al Maryah Island is Abu Dhabi's financial free zone, home to ADGM, The
Galleria, and Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi, all within a five-minute walk of
each other. It is also the site of the capital's most talked-about branded
residential launch: Jumeirah Residences Al Maryah Island, a waterfront tower
with one- to five-bedroom apartments and penthouses starting around AED 4.7
to 5 million.
